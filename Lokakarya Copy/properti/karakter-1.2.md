## 0. Jas Almamater USK (Referensi Pakaian Mahasiswa)

Dipakai oleh **Nadia dan Budi** saja di semua adegan. Mahasiswa figuran **tidak** memakai jas. Generate dulu, lalu lampirkan hasilnya sebagai referensi pakaian saat membuat Nadia dan Budi.

**Lampiran saat generate:** 2 foto jas (yang sudah kamu siapkan) + logo USK.

**Prompt Jas (3:4)**
```
Use the attached photos as the exact reference for the blazer, and the attached USK logo as the exact reference for the emblem. Photorealistic product photograph of the teal (turquoise-blue) university alma mater blazer of Universitas Syiah Kuala, shown on an invisible ghost mannequin (no person, no face, no head, no neck, no hands visible), front view, buttoned neatly, with a plain white collared dress shirt visible at the collar. Notched lapels, single-breasted two-button front, a chest pocket on the left with the golden star-shaped USK emblem embroidered exactly as in the logo reference, clean tailoring with natural fabric folds, matte polyester texture. Pure white studio background, soft even studio lighting, sharp focus, high detail, centered composition. Aspect ratio 3:4.
Negative prompt: person, face, head, hands, mannequin visible, model, cartoon, illustration, 3D render, wrong color, distorted logo, altered emblem, extra text, different logo, wrinkled mess, busy background, shadows on background, watermark, blurry.
```

**Opsional (tampak belakang)** — gunakan jika ingin referensi punggung untuk panel belakang di Prompt B:
```
Use the previously generated blazer image as the exact reference. Same teal USK alma mater blazer on an invisible ghost mannequin (no person, no face, no head, no hands), back view, plain back with a center vent, no emblem on the back, white collared shirt visible at the neckline. Pure white studio background, soft even studio lighting, sharp focus, high detail. Aspect ratio 3:4.
Negative prompt: person, face, head, hands, model, cartoon, illustration, 3D render, wrong color, added logo or text on the back, busy background, watermark, blurry.
```

**Cara pakai jas sebagai referensi karakter:** lampirkan gambar jas ini bersama prompt karakter mahasiswa. Prompt Nadia, Budi, dan figuran sudah memuat kalimat "exactly as in the attached blazer reference".
